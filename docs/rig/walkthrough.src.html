Why

Water path

  • The jug sits on the stand, raised so water siphons down.
  • Two tubes run from the jug to each glass column.
  • Each pair passes under a servo lever that pinches it shut.

Sensors into the Arduino

  • Heat sensor: room temperature and humidity, pin D2.
  • Ultrasonic meter over each column: water level, pins D4 to D7.
  • Servo valves on D9 and D10; amber lights on D11 and D12.

Arduino to laptop

  • One USB cable. Chrome talks to the board directly, no Wi-Fi.
  • Board to app: temperature, both water levels, valve states.
  • App to board: how long to open each valve, hour by hour.

Levels and figures are the design targets: 1 mL in a column stands for 1,500 gallons, the hybrid's worst day reaches about 593 mL, Max Day's cap is 347 mL. The real numbers come from the engine and the on-site calibration.
