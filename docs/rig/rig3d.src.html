On the bench

  • Gallon jug on a 25 cm stand: the county supply
  • Two straws through the jug's sides, each on a servo
  • Two clear cups, red line at one soda can (355 mL)
  • Ultrasonic meter over each cup

Into the Arduino

  • Hybrid servo D9, Max Day servo D10 (orange signal wires)
  • Meters: trig and echo on D4, D5 and D6, D7
  • Joystick forward axis A0; Run button D3
  • Servo power from a USB charger on the breadboard rail, grounds tied

To the laptop

  • One USB cable, Arduino to laptop
  • The Max Day app in Chrome talks to the board directly
  • Board sends cup levels; app sends each straw's open time
  • No Wi-Fi needed

Illustration of the physical demo. The red line on each cup is one 12 oz soda can, 355 mL, standing for the 0.52 MGD cap (1 mL ≈ 1,465 gallons). The day's curves are shaped by hand here; on the real rig the Max Day engine sets them from measured 2025 Ashland weather.
