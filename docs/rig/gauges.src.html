Two water meters, one hot day

Two servo needles on a board show how much water two identical data centers use on the hottest day of the year. A judge holds a hot cup to the heat sensor. The hybrid's needle swings into the red. Max Day's stops at the line.

Max Day

  1. Press the button

    A real push button on the board starts last June's hottest day, sped up to 30 seconds.

  2. Hand over the hot cup

    “You're the heat wave.” The heat sensor reads it live and both plants react.

  3. Watch the needles

    The hybrid gulps water all afternoon and swings into the red zone.

  4. Max Day holds the line

    Same heat, same day. It switches to air cooling in time and stops at the cap.

What you need (all owned)

  • Arduino and USB cable
  • 2 servos for the needles
  • 1 heat sensor
  • 1 push button (or the joystick's click)
  • Cardboard or foam board, 2 straws

What plugs in where

  • Hybrid servo: pin 9
  • Max Day servo: pin 10
  • Heat sensor: pin 2
  • Button: pin 3 and ground
  • Arduino USB to the laptop

Build

  • About 45 minutes, $0
  • Print two dial faces, glue to the board
  • Servo behind each dial, straw as the needle
  • I write the Arduino code and the app link
  • No water anywhere

This page is an illustration of the demo. The needle curves here are shaped by hand; the real ones come from the Max Day engine running on measured 2025 Ashland weather. End values (0.89 MGD for the hybrid, 0.52 for Max Day, 42% lower) are from the handoff's engine run.
