On the bench

  • Gallon jug on a 25 cm stand: the county supply
  • Two straws through the jug's sides, each on a servo
  • Two clear cups, red line at one soda can (355 mL)
  • Ultrasonic meter over each cup

Into the Raspberry Pi

  • Hybrid servo GPIO18, Max Day servo GPIO13 (orange signal wires)
  • Meters: trig and echo on GPIO23, 24 and GPIO5, 6, echo through a 1 kΩ and 2 kΩ divider
  • Run button GPIO27; joystick press (held = heat wave) GPIO17
  • Power bank feeds the Pi and the servo rail; grounds tied

The laptop

  • Joins the Pi's own "MaxDay" Wi-Fi, no venue network needed
  • The Pi runs the plant controller on its own
  • The Max Day app can mirror the plant live
  • The rig still runs if the laptop is closed
